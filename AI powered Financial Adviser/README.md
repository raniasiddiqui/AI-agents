***AI-powered Financial Advisor***

FinAdvisor is an AI-driven financial advisor that provides real-time investment insights and diversified portfolio recommendations using LangChain, Google Gemini, and financial market APIs.

It analyzes stocks, bonds, ETFs, mutual funds, and currencies, then generates an adaptive investment allocation based on your risk tolerance (low, medium, or high).

It’s ideal for financial dashboards, currency tracking tools, and educational or analytical applications that need up-to-date USD market information without relying on paid APIs.

🚀 Features

- ✅ AI-Powered Insights — Uses Google Gemini (langchain_google_genai) for expert-level financial reasoning.
- ✅ Live Market Data — Integrates with:

- Yahoo Finance (yfinance) for real-time stock, ETF, and bond yields.

- Frankfurter.dev for live USD exchange rates.

- Google News RSS for the latest market headlines.
- ✅ Dynamic Portfolio Allocation — Provides a table-based breakdown of investments with percentages, dollar amounts, and rationale.
- ✅ Risk-Based Recommendations — Adapts to user-defined risk levels.
